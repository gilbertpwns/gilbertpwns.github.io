# Resource Development & Weaponization

* In the context of red teaming and penetration testing, resource development and weaponization are two crucial phases that occur during the lifecycle of an attack/pentest.
* The terms "resource development" and "weaponization" organization in military strategy, they have been adapted and applied in cybersecurity to describe key phrases in the lifecycle of cybersecurity to describe key phases in the lifecycle of cyberattacks.
* Resource development focuses on acquiring or *building the neccesary resources* for an attack, while weaponization involves *turning those resources into effective cyber weapons*.
