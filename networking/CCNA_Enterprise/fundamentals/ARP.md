# Address Resolution Protocol

ARP is to resolve IP addresses to MAC addresses.

0x0806

An ARP request will metaphorically be something like this, "I'm looking for the MAC of 2.2.2.1) target address. I am 2.2.2.2 and my MAC is 11.11.1x.xx.xx."

An ARP reply of with the correct IP target address will provide it's MAC address.

You *only* ARP for devices on your subnet.
