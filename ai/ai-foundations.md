## why prompt engineering matters

## AI as a Probabilistic System

* AI predicts likely responses
* multiple valid answers may exists
* prompt wording influences probabilities
* better guidance produces more reliable output 

##  incorrect mindsets

* the 'search engine fallacy'
    + interacting with AI the same as with a search engine
* the 'one-shot' expectation
    + expectigng a perfect response from the very first prompt
* the 'magic wand  illusion'
    + expecting AI to read your mind beyond what you explicitly tell it

## characteristics of a good prompt 

* define a clear objective
* provide relevant centext
* state specific expectations
* remove unnecessary ambiguity
* include appropriate details

## S.C.O.P.E Framework

* a practical framework for organizing prompts
    + situation: provide background and relevant context for the task
    + character: define the role or persona th AI should assume
    + output: specificy the desired format, structure, or type of output
    + purpose: clearly state the goal using an action verb for the task
    + examples: provide examples to show the AI the pattern to follow
